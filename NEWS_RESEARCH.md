# News research release 1

Periodic metadata collection: `.github/workflows/news.yml` runs twice hourly and after market collection. RSS availability limits coverage; this is not a comprehensive news wire. Titles and source links only, no copied article bodies. Public issuer newsroom sources have a source-bound ticker; exact company title matches are explicitly candidates. Ambiguous or unknown entities stay in review. Curate `data/news/reviews.json` using `articleId`, `verifiedTickers`, and optionally timezone-qualified `officialAt` plus `officialSourceUrl`. Never infer the earliest official announcement from a later news timestamp. Source published time is the earliest retrieved publisher time, not a guaranteed global first announcement.

Canonical links deduplicate feed copies. Conservative exact/near title clusters require the same companies, dates, categories and numerals. Substantially rewritten reports remain separate until manual review; no claim of complete semantic deduplication. Preserve publisher updated time only when supplied, and keep firstSeen/lastSeen plus metadata revisions and immutable collection snapshots. No title sentiment model.

Market snapshots archive once per country/date. Future collectors also preserve versioned per-symbol OHLCV observations under data/ohlcv. Older chart history lacks daily ratio20 and OHLCV in places: those remain unavailable. This archive is not an institutionally certified point-in-time dataset. Corporate-action adjustments and prices must be validated before strategy backtesting.

Event windows: previous five sessions and response session +1/+3/+5, relative to pre-event close. First response session uses regular local closing times and explicit exchange calendar validity and holidays. Early-close/special sessions are not yet supported. Intraday EOD includes pre-announcement activity. Missing/estimated data remain missing. Peer comparison uses original same-industry grouping, excludes issuer, and requires 5 paired peers. Market is the ex-issuer tracking universe mean, not an official market index. Historical memberships and event causality are not established.

Content outlines are deterministic editorial templates, not automatic financial conclusions. Cohort matching, announcement materiality extraction, comprehensive official filings, and out-of-sample incremental validation are not yet implemented. Cohort sizes and performance are deliberately not fabricated. No new combined score.
